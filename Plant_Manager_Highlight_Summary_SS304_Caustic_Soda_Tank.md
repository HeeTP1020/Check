# Plant Manager Highlight Summary

**Project:** 2 × 1,600 m³ SS304 Caustic Soda Tanks, 91 Sakra Avenue, Jurong Island  
**Purpose:** Quick management summary of the independent review  
**Date:** 8 June 2026

---

## Bottom Line

**Do not approve PO yet.** The quotation report is useful for price comparison, but it is **not safe or complete enough for procurement approval**.

The biggest concern is that the report recommends Falkor/FES mainly on price, while major statutory, technical, safety, and contract gaps remain open.

---

## Key Red Flags

| Area | Main Issue | Plant Manager Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Regulatory | SCDF, NEA, JTC, PUB, MOM, BCA, LTA, NParks submissions not properly identified | Project may be stopped or delayed by authorities |
| Foundation | 20 piles/tank not justified by soil investigation | Settlement or foundation failure risk |
| Tank Design | No formal design basis; tank dimensions/capacity need confirmation | Tank may not meet operating capacity or code |
| NDT / Welding | Falkor PT-only NDT is not acceptable for API 650 shell welds | Weld defects may go undetected |
| Process Safety | No API 2350 overfill, HAZOP, bund-volume check, emergency venting | Chemical spill / safety incident risk |
| Commercial | No clear LD, warranty, insurance, performance guarantee | Client carries cost and schedule risk |

---

## Decision Recommendation

**Pause procurement. Do not issue PO until the following are closed in writing:**

1. Authority submission plan for SCDF, NEA, JTC, PUB, MOM and BCA.
2. Full design basis memorandum.
3. Geotechnical investigation and PE-endorsed foundation design.
4. API 650 compliance confirmation, including RT/UT, vacuum box test, WPS/PQR, MTRs.
5. Process safety package: HAZOP/HAZID, overfill protection, bund sizing, emergency venting, PSSR.
6. Formal EPCC contract with LD, warranty, insurance, performance guarantees and third-party inspection.
