# Independent Technical, Regulatory, Constructability, and Procurement Review

**Subject report reviewed:** `SS304 Caustic Soda Storage Tank — Quotation Comparison & Technical Review Report`
**Project:** 2 Nos. SS304 Caustic Soda Storage Tanks, 1,600 m³ each, 91 Sakra Avenue, Jurong Island, Singapore
**Review date:** 8 June 2026
**Review status:** Independent critical review for procurement decision support
**Reviewer position:** Independent technical / regulatory / procurement review; not a statutory submission and not a substitute for Singapore QP/PE certification.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

### 1.1 Overall Opinion

The current quotation comparison report is **useful as a preliminary commercial and technical screening document**, but it is **not sufficient for procurement approval or PO award** for a Jurong Island caustic soda tank project without major additional clarifications, engineering deliverables, authority-submission planning, and contractual protections.

The report correctly identifies several critical vendor gaps, especially Falkor/FES shell-weld NDT, pile design uncertainty, missing WPS/PQR/MTRs, passivation ambiguity, and missing warranty/insurance terms. However, the report still does **not** adequately cover Singapore statutory approval pathways, dangerous-goods / hazardous-substance licensing, SCDF/NEA/PUB/JTC interfaces, Design-for-Safety obligations, temporary works, lifting and construction WSH controls, process-safety philosophy, civil/geotechnical design basis, or E&I/instrumentation requirements.

### 1.2 Procurement Recommendation

**Do not issue an unconditional PO based on the report.**

A conditional letter of intent or clarification phase may proceed only if the client first issues a formal technical specification and requires the selected vendor to return a compliant technical proposal, authority-submission matrix, execution plan, inspection/test plan, and full commercial qualifications list.

### 4.2 Geometry and Capacity Concern

The report now notes FES slide dimensions of **11,450 mm ID** and **15,400 mm height**. A simple cylinder check gives:

- Internal radius = 5.725 m.
- Gross cylinder area = π × 5.725² ≈ 103.0 m².
- Gross cylinder volume at 15.4 m = about 1,586 m³.

This is below the stated 1,600 m³ before considering freeboard, unusable bottom volume, roof geometry, nozzle elevations and operating margin. Therefore, the reported geometry is not a complete design basis and may be inconsistent with the required working capacity.

**Required action:** Require the vendor to submit the API 650 data sheet and a certified capacity calculation showing nominal, working, overflow, high-high, high and low operating volumes.

## 11. Final Opinion on Procurement Approval

### 11.1 Is the Report Sufficient for Procurement Approval?

**No.** The report is a strong first-pass quotation comparison, but it is **not yet sufficient** for procurement approval because it does not establish a complete design basis, statutory approval route, process safety basis, geotechnical basis, or contractually enforceable vendor obligations.
